Key observation:
Operational reliability and recent UI changes are driving user errors and frustration, while many users are unclear about the full set of statutory and platform charges applied to trades.

Action ideas:
1) Prioritise fixes for data-loading and execution reliability (blank screens, order failures) and publish incident-status updates.
2) Revert or make configurable the recent UI change that places the scalper option where users press accidentally; add guard/confirmation for high-risk buttons.
3) Add a clear fee breakdown at order confirmation and in post-trade statements showing brokerage plus each statutory and platform charge.

Users cannot tell what makes up the total deducted on a trade beyond the quoted brokerage (statutory taxes and other charges). Secondary: The official pages reviewed do not list a charge on cancelled or unexecuted orders.

Fee explainer:
- Brokerage: Equity brokerage is Rs.20 or 0.1% per executed order, whichever is lower; minimum Rs.5 per executed order (Groww pricing page).
- STT & Stamp Duty: For equity delivery trades, STT is 0.100% on buy and 0.100% on sell; for sales settled otherwise than by delivery, STT is 0.025% on the seller. Stamp duty on transfer of security other than debenture is 0.015% on the buyer for delivery trades and 0.003% on the buyer for non-delivery trades (NSE page).
- Exchange & SEBI charges: Exchange transaction charges are NSE: 0.00297% and BSE: 0.00375% (apply on buy and sell); SEBI turnover charge is 0.0001% (apply on buy and sell) (Groww pricing page / NSE page).
- DP charges on delivery sells: Depository charges are Rs.3.5 (male) and Rs.3.25 (female); Groww lists Rs.16.5 for Groww charges on sell-side DP entries (Groww pricing page).
- GST: 18% GST applies on brokerage, DP charges (per sell transaction on delivery trades), exchange transaction charges and SEBI turnover charge; GST is payable by stock brokers and collected from clients (NSE page / Groww pricing page).
- The official pages reviewed do not list a charge on cancelled or unexecuted orders.
